## 2. Card Formulation Rules (Specific to MBB 321)

### A. Biochemical Reactions: The "3-Cloze Equation Rule"
- **Never** test an entire reaction equation as a single massive blank (`{{c1::A + B -> C + D}}`).
- **Never** test arbitrary step index numbers (`Step {{c2::6}}`).
- Format every pathway reaction as a visual equation on a single note with up to **3 functional cloze groups**:
  - **`c1` (Carbon Pathway):** Main carbon substrate(s) and product(s)
  - **`c2` (Catalyst):** Enzyme name inside brackets over the arrow: `——[{{c2::Enzyme}}]——>`
  - **`c3` (Energetics / Cofactors):** Energy carriers, coenzymes, and inorganic molecules (`ATP`, `ADP`, `NAD⁺`, `NADH + H⁺`, `FAD`, `FADH₂`, `Pi`, `CO₂`, `H₂O`)

**Standard Reaction Template:**
```html
[Pathway Name] (Reaction X):<br>{{c1::Substrate}} + {{c3::ATP/Cofactor}} ——[{{c2::Enzyme}}]——> {{c1::Product}} + {{c3::ADP/NADH}}
```

### B. Deep Mechanistic & Thermodynamic Cards
Always inspect both the slide text and handwritten margin annotations for:
1. **Active-Site Mechanisms & Cofactors:**
   - Specific catalytic amino acid residues (e.g., *Cys149 thiohemiacetal -> thioester in GAPDH*, *phosphorylated His in PGM*, *Lys/Glu in Enolase*, *Asp/His in TPI*).
   - Role of metal ions like $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ (shielding negative charges on $\beta$- and $\gamma$-phosphates, stabilizing enolate/dianion intermediates).
   - Kinetic mechanisms (e.g., *Random Bi–Bi*, *diffusion-limited catalytic perfection*).
2. **Thermodynamics ($\Delta G'^\circ$ vs. $\Delta G'$):**
   - How standard-state endergonic reactions ($\Delta G'^\circ > 0$) proceed forward in the cell ($\Delta G' \approx 0$ or $< 0$) via rapid product depletion (keeping the mass-action ratio $Q$ low).
   - High-energy phosphoryl-transfer potentials (e.g., acyl-phosphate in 1,3-BPG at $-49.0\text{ kJ/mol}$ and enol-phosphate in PEP at $-61.9\text{ kJ/mol}$ vs. ATP at $-30.5\text{ kJ/mol}$).
   - Tautomerization (e.g., *enol-to-keto pyruvate*) and resonance stabilization.
3. **Regulation & Pathway Commitment:**
   - Irreversible control points ($\Delta G'^\circ \ll 0$).
   - Allosteric activators (R state) and inhibitors (T state) and product inhibition.
4. **Redox Balance & Stoichiometry:**
   - Internal disproportionation (extracting free energy with no net change in carbon oxidation state).
   - Coenzyme pool regeneration (e.g., lactate and ethanol fermentation regenerating $\text{NAD}^+$ for GAPDH).
   - Net balanced equations and ATP accounting (invested vs. recovered vs. net payoff).

### C. The "Support-Only" Extra Field Rule & Extra Extraction Principle
- **Support-Only Extra:** The `extra` field exists strictly to support the tested clozes (via diagram grounding `<img src="...">`, coenzyme pool connections, or a 1-sentence thermodynamic takeaway). It must NEVER introduce new, untested reactions, intermediate structures, or catalytic residues.
- **The Testability Axiom:** If a catalytic residue, cofactor role, or thermodynamic shift is substantive enough to appear on an exam, it MUST have its own active-recall Cloze card.
- **Extra Extraction Check:** Before finalizing `extra`, ask: *"Does this contain an untested reaction mechanism, enzyme regulation fact, or energetic nuance?"* If YES, extract it out into a dedicated card and keep `extra` strictly supportive!
